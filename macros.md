== Notes ==
* Macros are permitted according to the official Sol's RNG Discord server.
** Although fishing macros are not ban able, the developers of Sol's RNG have explicitly stated that they find the use of fishing macros an "unfair advantage" and discourage players from using them.
* Macros allow players to progress without being actively at their device.
* If no listed macro works, users can record their own using TinyTask (although not recommended).
** Item spawn locations can be found on the [[Items]] page.
** [[Overture|{{Aura|Overture}}]] and [[Overture : History|{{Aura|Overture : History}}]] can disrupt macros if PvP is enabled.
